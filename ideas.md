# SIH26161 Visual Direction

## Chosen direction: Operations room calm

A focused emergency-operations dashboard: dark navy navigation, light data panels, cool blue flood accents, and restrained severity colors for risk states. The visual language should feel like a decision surface for a control room rather than a marketing site.

- **Design movement:** editorial data-dense control room interface with clear hierarchy.
- **Core principles:** make the next action obvious, keep source/provenance visible, use severity color sparingly, favor comparison over decoration.
- **Color philosophy:** navy foundation (#0b1630), blue/cyan operational accents (#2563eb, #22d3ee), ink (#14213d), warm canvas (#f4f7fb), semantic green/amber/orange/red for Low/Medium/High/Critical.
- **Layout paradigm:** compact left navigation, wide content canvas, grid-based KPI cards, split map/table views, sticky utility/filter controls.
- **Signature elements:** persistent DEMO/SIMULATION DATA strip, floodwave mark, thin contour-like dividers, map-grid background, small uppercase section labels.
- **Interaction philosophy:** every control produces an observable update; use tooltips, progressive disclosure, and inline explanations instead of modal-heavy flows.
- **Animation:** subtle slide/fade for panels, a short simulation playback curve, no decorative looping motion; respect reduced motion.
- **Typography system:** Inter/system sans for UI, tabular numbers for metrics, compact all-caps labels at 11–12px, sentence case for actions.
- **Brand essence:** precise, calm, transparent, action-oriented.
- **Brand voice:** operational and honest: “DEMO/SIMULATION DATA”, “Recommended by dataset”, “Not HEC-RAS output”.
- **Wordmark/logo:** simple floodwave passing a dam gate, built from two or three flat shapes with negative space.
- **Signature brand color:** electric cyan on deep navy.

No additional imagery is needed beyond the required project logo because this is a functional data/control dashboard.
